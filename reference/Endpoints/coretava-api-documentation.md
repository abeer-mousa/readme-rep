## About Coretava

At Coretava, we're redefining how retailers and brands connect, engage, and grow by uniting AI-powered **CRM** and **Retail Media (Ads)** into a single, intelligent ecosystem.

Our **CoreAds** platform provides the building blocks to:

* **Create personalized customer experiences** that drive engagement and retention.
* **Launch data-driven campaigns** with precision targeting and real-time optimization.
* **Connect media and marketing efforts** across all customer touchpoints.
* **Unify data** between shoppers, brands, and retailers in one place.

Whether you're integrating customer data, syncing products and catalogs, managing campaigns, or tracking conversions, Coretava enables you to:

* Turn insights into actionable strategies
* Transform automation into meaningful engagement
* Build lasting relationships that scale with your business

**Start building with Coretava** — where every API call helps you connect smarter, engage deeper, and grow faster.
